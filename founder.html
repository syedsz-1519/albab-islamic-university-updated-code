Adnan Al Farooq

Educator, Researcher & Public Speaker

Adnan Al Farooq is an educator and content creator dedicated to preserving and contextualizing Islamic scholarship for contemporary audiences. As the founder and director of Albab Islamic University, he leads research initiatives, public talks, and educational programmes bridging classical Islamic sciences with modern challenges.

Vision

To create a generation of thinking Muslims: people who understand their faith deeply, engage with the world rigorously, and contribute to their communities with wisdom and compassion.
